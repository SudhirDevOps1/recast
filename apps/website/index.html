Built like a studio, priced like nothing

Everything Recast does happens locally. The only network calls it ever makes are the ones you explicitly approve — checking for updates and downloading models or codecs you ask for.

Auto-zoom camera work

Interest clustering finds where you click, then critically damped springs fly the camera — subtle 1.25×, balanced 1.6× or punchy 2.0×. Manual keyframes and follow-cursor mode when you want control.

Crash-safe by design

Recordings land on disk as 2-second segments with an atomic manifest. A crash costs you a fragment, never a take — sessions recover automatically.

beta

Local captions

Whisper runs on your machine — no audio ever uploaded. Burn captions in or export .srt / .vtt. English and Hindi first.

Export everything

MP4 (H.264) by default; WebM, HEVC, ProRes and GIF when you need them. The used encoder path is always shown, and every export is verified with ffprobe.

Private by default

No account. No analytics. No crash reports unless you opt in. Projects save as open, documented .recast files you can inspect.

Reusable style presets

Six built-in looks: frame, padding, background, shadow, cursor style. Save any look as a .recaststyle file and share it with your team.

Keyboard-first, ⌘K everywhere

Space, J/K/L, I/O, S to split, full undo history — plus a command palette that puts every action one keystroke away.

Native on all three OSes

Custom title bar, global hotkeys, tray control, per-monitor DPI, Wayland-portal honesty — it feels like your platform, not a web view.

In the workshop: live annotations, face-cam picture-in-picture, tracked redaction and word-level caption timing. Follow along in STATUS.md — it always tells the truth about what ships today.

Three steps, no timeline

1

Record

Pick a screen, a window or an area. Mic and system audio on separate tracks, countdown, pause/resume, global hotkeys. Cursor sampled at 60 Hz in the background.

2

Let Recast cut

Auto-zoom writes the camera moves from your clicks. Trim with I/O, choose a preset, set the aspect — the preview is the export, pixel for pixel.

3

Export

MP4 in H.264 with a size estimate before you commit, cancel any time without partial files, and an ffprobe check after every render.

Questions

Is it really free? What's the catch?

There is no catch and no paid tier. Recast is GPL-3.0-or-later and community-maintained. The whole product — recorder, editor, every export format — is free.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Recording, editing and exporting are fully offline. Recast only touches the network if you ask it to: check for updates, download a Whisper model, or fetch a missing FFmpeg binary.

Why does my computer warn me when I open it?

The installers are not code-signed yet (certificates are in progress — see docs/code-signing.md). On Windows, click "More info → Run anyway". On macOS, right-click the app and choose Open. Every release publishes SHA256SUMS so you can verify what you downloaded.

How is this different from Screen Studio or Cap?

Same cinematic auto-zoom idea, but there is no subscription, no account, no upload and no watermark. Your recordings never leave your machine, and the project format is an open, documented file.
